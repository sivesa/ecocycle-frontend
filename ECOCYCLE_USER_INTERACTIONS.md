# EcoCycle: Human-Level System Interactions

EcoCycle is a digital bridge that connects households, independent waste collectors, and recycling plants into a seamless circular economy. This document explains how people use the system and how the platform sustains itself.

---

## 1. The User Journeys

### 🏠 The Household (The Generator)
For a household, EcoCycle turns "trash" into a digital asset.
1.  **Onboarding**: The resident signs up and pays a one-time **R150 activation fee**. This ensures only committed participants enter the ecosystem.
2.  **Segregation & Logging**: Instead of throwing everything in one bin, the resident separates plastic, metal, and glass. They log these items in the app, noting the estimated volume (e.g., "10kg of PET bottles").
3.  **The Request**: When their recycling bin is full, they "Declare for Collection." The app notifies nearby verified collectors.
4.  **The Handshake (Security)**: When a collector arrives, the household app generates a **unique 6-digit OTP**. The resident gives this to the collector only when they are satisfied the collector is present and ready.
5.  **Rewards**: Once the collector weighs and confirms the load, the household receives points or cash in their **In-App Wallet**, which can be withdrawn or used for rewards.

### 🚛 The Collector (The Entrepreneur)
For a collector, EcoCycle is a business management tool that provides a steady stream of "inventory."
1.  **Business Setup**: The collector defines their "turf" (service areas) and what they accept (e.g., "I only take glass and metal"). They also pay the **R150 activation fee** to be a verified partner.
2.  **Job Discovery**: The collector sees a map of nearby households with ready-to-collect waste. They "Accept" a request to lock it in.
3.  **Verification**: Upon arrival, they enter the household's **OTP** into their app. This proves they performed the service at the correct location.
4.  **Measurement**: The collector weighs the bags. They enter the **actual weight** (e.g., "12.5kg") into the app. The system immediately calculates the payout based on the current market rate.
5.  **Earnings**: The collector's wallet is updated. They can see their daily earnings, fuel efficiency, and "EcoScore" (reputation).

### 👑 The EcoCycle Admin (The Market Maker)
The Admin ensures the ecosystem is healthy, fair, and profitable.
1.  **Pricing Control**: Admins update the global **Price per KG** for materials (e.g., adjusting for a global surge in aluminum prices). This keeps the platform competitive.
2.  **Verification**: They review and approve new collectors, ensuring vehicle inspections and IDs are valid to maintain trust.
3.  **Market Oversight**: They monitor "heatmaps" to see which areas are producing the most waste and where more collectors might be needed.
4.  **Dispute Resolution**: If a household claims a collector didn't show up or provided the wrong weight, the Admin reviews the transaction audit logs.

## 2. Revenue Model: How the System Wins

EcoCycle is designed to be a "profit-driven social enterprise." Revenue is generated through three primary channels:

### A. One-Time Activation Fees
Every participant (Household and Collector) pays a flat **R150 activation fee**. 
*   **Purpose**: This covers the cost of identity verification (KYC), initial support, and prevents "spam" accounts.
*   **System View**: This provides immediate working capital for the platform to scale its infrastructure.

### B. The Transaction "Spread"
The system acts as a middleman between the independent collector and the large-scale industrial recycling plant.
*   **The Buy Price**: The system sets a price for the collector (e.g., R4.00/kg for Aluminum).
*   **The Sell Price**: EcoCycle aggregates tons of verified, high-quality material and sells it to industrial plants at a bulk rate (e.g., R5.50/kg).
*   **The Spread**: The system captures the **R1.50/kg difference** as a service fee for facilitating the logistics, verification, and payment.

### C. Data & ESG Analytics
Corporations and Municipalities are under pressure to report on "Waste Diversion" (how much trash didn't go to a landfill).
*   **Verification as a Service**: Because every KG in EcoCycle is verified by an OTP and a GPS-tagged transaction, the data is highly valuable.
*   **Revenue**: EcoCycle sells anonymized recycling trend reports and ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) certificates to corporate partners who want to prove their environmental impact.

### D. Corporate Sponsorship & Gamification
Brands (like Coca-Cola or SAB) want to ensure their packaging is recycled.
*   **Sponsored Rewards**: Brands pay EcoCycle to "boost" rewards for their specific products (e.g., "Earn double points for recycling Brand X bottles").
*   **Revenue**: EcoCycle takes a management fee for running these targeted recycling campaigns.

## 3. The "Trust Loop" Summary
1.  **Household** gets paid to segregate.
2.  **Collector** gets a digital route and guaranteed inventory.
3.  **Recycler** gets clean, pre-sorted bulk material.
4.  **EcoCycle** takes a cut of every verified KG that moves through the loop.
